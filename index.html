01 biography

Jeonghyun Sim is an M.S. student in Information Security at the Hacking and Countermeasure Research Lab (HCRL), Korea University, advised by Prof. Huy Kang Kim. Jeonghyun’s research focuses on multi-agent systems, AI security, AI for security, and trustworthy machine learning, with current work on making LLM-based multi-agent automation secure and reliable, from intent alignment in agent reasoning to defenses against jailbreaks and prompt injections.

Jeonghyun received a B.S. in Industrial Security from Dankook University and conducted research at the Intelligent Security Technology Lab (ISTL) there.

02 research interests

Multi-Agent Systems

Intent alignment and detecting intentional misalignment in the reasoning of LLM multi-agent systems.

AI Security

Auditing LLM vulnerabilities, including jailbreaks and prompt injections, and defensive alignment.

AI for Security

Applying machine learning to security problems, from vehicle network intrusion detection to automated vulnerability detection.

Trustworthy Machine Learning

Adversarial robustness of vision models, from attack detection to restoration for autonomous driving perception.
